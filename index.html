Valuation discussion

Illustrative financial model, not an appraisal or transaction comp analysis. EBITDA is not distributable cash. No salary or commissions to the new partner are modeled. Returns exclude investor-level taxes, dilution, seller financing and earn-outs. Valuation multiples are user-selected assumptions, not verified market comparables.
